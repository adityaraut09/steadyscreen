Before you start

For a supervising adult running the CDC STEADI 30-Second Chair Stand. The app gives the “Go”, times 30 seconds and counts full stands from the camera, so you can watch the participant. Afterwards you check each counted or doubtful rise against still images. It flags many situations where it could not measure reliably — not all of them — so every count must be checked.

What it does not do: it does not diagnose or predict falls, and it shows the CDC comparison table only after the count has been checked.

  3. Practice stand: stand up fully with arms crossed, hold still for about 1 second, sit down.
